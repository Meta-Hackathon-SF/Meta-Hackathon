## Show the intelligence
A short creative activity should satisfy several real constraints. Show how a new duration/time constraint changes the plan. Don't describe code noticing a threshold as Muse reasoning. Show valid public explanation, not private chain-of-thought or leaked constraints.

## Q&A
Why Facebook? The proposed feature activates existing interest communities and connects them to shared activities. Our integration is simulated in this prototype.
Without AI? Fixed templates cover simple cases. The demo tests interpretation and adaptation across differing constraints.
Trust? Opt-in participation, public catalog venues, size cap, authorized room data, leave controls, reconfirmation.
Why return? A live follow-up and explicit hosting action demonstrate the mechanism; retention impact is not established.
Why not Groups alone? A discussion surface still leaves participants to form a small team and negotiate constraints. Our feature proposes and revises that shared activity.

## Thirty-second pitch
Joining a Facebook Group is easy. Feeling like you belong takes more. Group Quests helps young adults turn shared interests into shared experiences. Tell it what you would enjoy in a voice note. Powered by Muse, it forms a small team, plans an activity around everyone's preferences, and adapts when plans change. Next week, it helps someone take the lead. Facebook already has the communities. We help people become part of them.
